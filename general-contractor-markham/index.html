Start with the scope of your renovation

Hiring a general contractor in Markham can make sense when a project includes several trades, changes to a room’s layout, or decisions about finishes and materials. Before choosing a contractor, get clear about what you want changed and what matters most to you. A kitchen update, a bathroom renovation, and a whole-house project can each call for a different plan.

Write down the rooms involved, the changes you have in mind, and any questions you want answered. If you’re still deciding on the layout or finish choices, look for a team that can help with planning as well as construction. That support can help you understand the project before work gets underway.

What to prepare before you hire

Use this short checklist when you’re getting ready to talk with a general contractor in Markham:

  • List the rooms and areas you want included.
  • Note what you want to change and what should stay as it is.
  • Gather inspiration images or examples of finishes you like.
  • Write down questions about layout, materials, and planning.
  • Share any decisions you haven’t made yet, so they can be discussed early.

Ask how the team will guide the project from planning into construction. A useful conversation should help you understand the steps involved and identify where more decisions are needed. Avoid choosing finishes too quickly or assuming every part of the plan is settled before discussing the full scope.
